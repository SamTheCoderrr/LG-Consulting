Meet Lyna

For close to twenty years, Lyna has worked at the intersection of hospitality and corporate events — building a network of trusted restaurant partners that most people simply don't have access to. She knows the kitchens, the chefs, and the operations behind them, so when she recommends a caterer for your event, it comes from firsthand experience, not a search engine.

LG Consulting was founded on a simple idea: companies shouldn't have to manage multiple vendors, chase invoices, or gamble on an unknown kitchen for an important event. Lyna acts as your single point of contact — on call from the first conversation through the last plate cleared — sourcing the right restaurant, coordinating every detail, and making sure nothing falls through.

When you work with LG Consulting, you're not hiring a faceless platform. You're getting direct access to someone who has spent two decades cultivating relationships with the best local restaurants and who treats your reputation like her own.
